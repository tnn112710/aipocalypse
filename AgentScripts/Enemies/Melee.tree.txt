# The goblins' tree: hunt a target (TargetSelector picks it: the player for now, fences, crops and animals later), chase,
# swing; when what it hunted died (the player), it stays where it is and dances; otherwise, with nothing to hunt
# (cheat.notarget), it wanders. The hunt's abort (Both) re-checks ChooseTarget every think: it cuts into the dance or the
# wander as soon as a target exists, and ends the hunt when none is left.
# Job: punish standing in the open at night; it runs at the player from wherever it spawned, from afar coming in from its
# own side (MeleeSettings.FlankAngle, straightening inside FlankFadeDistance) so a group fans out.
# Unaware: a raider goes for the farm (SpawnOrders.Objective): breaks in when the fence is shut, roams the yard once
# there (ObjectiveRadius); otherwise it wanders (a camp, cheat.notarget).
# Notices: the player within EnemyDefinition.SpotRange with a clear line (a fence is seen through), any hit, or a
# neighbour's alarm (AlertRadius); it shouts and turns on them. Takes its fighting stance and shouts within EngageRadius.
# Warns: a visible wind-up before each blow, which lands at HitTime and misses if the player stepped out of reach or arc.
# Gives up: when the target can't be targeted any more (dead, notarget), or LoseSightTime seconds after it last saw the
# player (then back to the farm, or it roams); at most AttackSlots' share swing at once, the rest wait on their own spot
# round the target at HoldDistance.
# Build: unity command script run --file .agents/skills/aipocalypse-enemy-ai/scripts/TreeKit.cs --entry TreeKit.Build
#        --args '["AgentScripts/Enemies/Melee.tree.txt"]'
# Rebuilding keeps the asset's GUID; edits made in the Behavior Designer window are overwritten, so make them here.
tree Assets/_Game/Content/Enemies/_Shared/Melee.asset
Repeater "Repeat forever"
  Selector "Hunt, else celebrate, else wander"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Hunt (aborts both ways)"
      ChooseTarget "Target?"
      ChaseTarget "Chase"
      MeleeAttack "Swing"
    Sequence "Celebrate the kill"
      TargetDefeated "Target died?"
      Celebrate "Dance in place"
    Sequence "Wander"
      ChooseWanderDestination "Choose reachable point"
      WalkToWanderDestination "Walk to point"
      PauseWander "Pause"
