# The goblins' tree: hunt a target (TargetSelector picks it: the player for now, fences, crops and animals later), chase,
# swing; wander when there is nothing to hunt (player dead, cheat.notarget). The hunt's abort (Both) re-checks ChooseTarget
# every think: it cuts into the wander as soon as a target exists, and ends the hunt when none is left.
# Job: punish standing in the open at night; it runs at the player from wherever it spawned.
# Notices: always knows its target (raiders are sent at it); takes its fighting stance and shouts within EngageRadius.
# Warns: a visible wind-up before each blow, which lands at HitTime and misses if the player stepped out of reach or arc.
# Gives up: when the target can't be targeted any more (dead, notarget); at most AttackSlots' share swing at once, the
# rest wait at HoldDistance.
# Build: unity command script run --file .agents/skills/aipocalypse-enemy-ai/scripts/TreeKit.cs --entry TreeKit.Build
#        --args '["AgentScripts/Enemies/Melee.tree.txt"]'
# Rebuilding keeps the asset's GUID; edits made in the Behavior Designer window are overwritten, so make them here.
tree Assets/_Game/Content/Enemies/_Shared/Melee.asset
Repeater "Repeat forever"
  Selector "Hunt, else wander"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Hunt (aborts both ways)"
      ChooseTarget "Target?"
      ChaseTarget "Chase"
      MeleeAttack "Swing"
    Sequence "Wander"
      ChooseWanderDestination "Choose reachable point"
      WalkToWanderDestination "Walk to point"
      PauseWander "Pause"
