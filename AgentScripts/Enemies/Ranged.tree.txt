# The ranged goblins' tree (spear throwers, archers): hunt a target, get to throwing distance, throw; dance when what it
# hunted died; wander with nothing to hunt. Same shape as Melee.tree.txt; the hunt's abort (Both) re-checks ChooseTarget
# every think.
# Job: punish standing still in the open, and standing behind the fence: it keeps its distance and throws, over the
# fence when it must. It never breaks a fence (its definition gives Fence no weight).
# Unaware: wanders (cheat.notarget).
# Notices: always knows its target (raiders are sent at it); takes its stance inside RangedSettings.MaxRange.
# Then: runs to its own stand-off distance (each life picks one in StandOff), backs away when the target comes inside
# MinRange, settles for anywhere inside MaxRange when a shut fence stops it.
# Warns: a wind-up of ReleaseTime seconds with its sound (a bow drawn, a grunt) before every throw; the projectile has
# a bright trail, a lob shows where it will land, and it whistles just before it passes the player.
# Path: a straight throw with a clear line, a lob at a random angle over what's in the way (or by chance). Aim: a random
# lead on the target's movement and a random error (Spread); no two throws alike.
# Gives up: when the target can't be targeted any more; a stagger cancels a wind-up.
# Build: unity command script run --file .agents/skills/aipocalypse-enemy-ai/scripts/TreeKit.cs --entry TreeKit.Build
#        --args '["AgentScripts/Enemies/Ranged.tree.txt"]'
# Rebuilding keeps the asset's GUID; edits made in the Behavior Designer window are overwritten, so make them here.
tree Assets/_Game/Content/Enemies/_Shared/Ranged.asset
Repeater "Repeat forever"
  Selector "Hunt, else celebrate, else wander"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Hunt (aborts both ways)"
      ChooseTarget "Target?"
      TakeRangedPosition "Get to throwing distance"
      RangedAttack "Throw"
    Sequence "Celebrate the kill"
      TargetDefeated "Target died?"
      Celebrate "Dance in place"
    Sequence "Wander"
      ChooseWanderDestination "Choose reachable point"
      WalkToWanderDestination "Walk to point"
      PauseWander "Pause"
