# The ranged goblins' tree (spear throwers, archers): hunt a target, get to throwing distance, throw; fight hand to hand
# when the target is on top of it; dance when what it hunted died; wander with nothing to hunt. Same shape as
# Melee.tree.txt; the hunt's abort (Both) re-checks ChooseTarget every think.
# Job: punish standing still in the open, and standing behind the fence: it keeps its distance and throws, over the
# fence when it must. It never breaks a fence (its definition gives Fence no weight).
# Unaware: a raider goes for the farm (SpawnOrders) and waits outside the fence facing it; otherwise it wanders (a camp,
# cheat.notarget).
# Notices: the player within EnemyDefinition.SpotRange with a clear line (a fence is seen through), any hit, or a
# neighbour's alarm (AlertRadius); it shouts. Takes its stance inside RangedSettings.MaxRange.
# Then: runs to its own stand-off distance (each life picks one in StandOff), backs away when the target comes inside
# MinRange, settles for anywhere inside MaxRange when a shut fence stops it. Inside MeleeRange it fights hand to hand
# with the kind's MeleeSettings instead (TargetInMeleeReach aborts both ways), and throws again once the target is off.
# Warns: a wind-up of ReleaseTime seconds with its sound (a bow drawn, a grunt) before every throw; the projectile has
# a bright trail, a lob shows where it will land, and it whistles just before it passes the player.
# Path: a straight throw with a clear line, a lob at a random angle over what's in the way (or by chance). Aim: a random
# lead on the target's movement and a random error (Spread); no two throws alike. A straight throw is held while one of
# its own side (Friends) stands in the line.
# Gives up: when the target can't be targeted any more, or LoseSightTime seconds after it last saw the player (then back
# to the farm, or it roams); a stagger cancels a wind-up.
# Build: unity command script run --file .agents/skills/aipocalypse-enemy-ai/scripts/TreeKit.cs --entry TreeKit.Build
#        --args '["AgentScripts/Enemies/Ranged.tree.txt"]'
# Rebuilding keeps the asset's GUID; edits made in the Behavior Designer window are overwritten, so make them here.
tree Assets/_Game/Content/Enemies/_Shared/Ranged.asset
Repeater "Repeat forever"
  Selector "Hunt, else celebrate, else wander"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Hunt (aborts both ways)"
      ChooseTarget "Target?"
      Selector "Fight"
        Sequence AbortType=Both "Hand to hand when it's on top of it"
          TargetInMeleeReach "Within MeleeRange?"
          ChaseTarget "Close in"
          MeleeAttack "Swing"
        Sequence "Throw"
          TakeRangedPosition "Get to throwing distance"
          RangedAttack "Throw"
    Sequence "Celebrate the kill"
      TargetDefeated "Target died?"
      Celebrate "Dance in place"
    Sequence "Wander"
      ChooseWanderDestination "Choose reachable point"
      WalkToWanderDestination "Walk to point"
      PauseWander "Pause"
