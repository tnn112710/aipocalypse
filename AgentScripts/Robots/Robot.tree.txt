# The robots' tree (Rob15 and any later model): friendly helpers at home who open the shop, upgrades and other menus, or
# (a RobotJob) work on their own. Job: no fight. Be lively and easy to find: mostly idle at its spot, now and then a stroll.
# Aware of: the player (RobotRoster: where they are, where they look, whether they hold a gun, whether they come home), the
# world clock's waking hours (RobotSettings.Awake), raids (RaidStarted / RaidEnded, a barrier broken). Never targeted.
# Told by the player: E or a look (greet), hold E (pat), the whistle (Robot.Call), walking into it (step), shooting it (a
# flinch; KnockdownHits in a row knock it over, then it sulks), a gun pointed at its head (nervous), too many whistles
# (annoyed: it sulks and ignores whistles for a while), walking out of the yard (a wave goodbye).
# Then: flinch or lie knocked over; step aside; hide from a raid (brave for a moment, then its shelter); answer a call
# (whistle or welcome home: react, trot to a meeting point inside the yard, wait, shrug, go home); get nervous; greet (wave
# or talk; a pat: heart eyes; sulking: a head shake); cheer or cry after a raid; see the player off (a wave); chat with
# another free robot close by (wave or talk, then answer); welcome the player home (look round,
# spot them, come over); at night yawn, walk slowly to its spot and stand by (morning: a hop); work (its job, if any);
# otherwise idle at its spot and sometimes stroll within its radius (never into a NoGo box) and back.
# Warns: beeps for each moment (RobotSettings.Sounds, limited by BeepGap / SharedBeepGap), faces on its screen.
# Gives up: a call when the wait runs out or the player walks away; a walk when it times out.
# Aborts: Both re-checks the conditional every think (a hit cuts into everything below it, and so on down). The step aside
# and the welcome clear their own request when they start, so they abort LowerPriority only (else they'd cancel themselves).
# Build: unity command script run --file .agents/skills/aipocalypse-enemy-ai/scripts/TreeKit.cs --entry TreeKit.Build
#        --args '["AgentScripts/Robots/Robot.tree.txt"]'
# Rebuilding keeps the asset's GUID; edits made in the Behavior Designer window are overwritten, so make them here.
tree Assets/_Game/Content/Robots/_Shared/Robot.asset
Repeater "Repeat forever"
  Selector "Flinch, step aside, hide, come, nervous, greet, cheer, see off, chat, welcome, standby, work, else home life"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Flinch or knocked down (aborts both ways)"
      RobotIsHit "Hit?"
      RobotFlinch "Stand, or lie and get up"
    Sequence AbortType=LowerPriority "Step aside (aborts lower)"
      RobotIsAskedToStep "Walked into?"
      RobotStepAside "Trot out of the way"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Hide from a raid (aborts both ways)"
      RobotIsScared "Raid?"
      RobotHide "Brave, then hide"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Answer a call (aborts both ways)"
      RobotIsCalled "Whistled or welcoming?"
      RobotAnswerCall "Come, wait, shrug"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Nervous (aborts both ways)"
      RobotIsNervous "Gun pointed at it?"
      RobotNervous "Worried, shake head"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Greet (aborts both ways)"
      RobotIsGreeting "Looked at, E or a pat?"
      RobotGreet "Face, wave, talk or love"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Cheer or cry (aborts both ways)"
      RobotIsCheering "Raid over?"
      RobotCelebrate "Win and dance, or cry"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "See the player off (aborts both ways)"
      RobotIsSeeingOff "Player left the yard?"
      RobotSeeOff "Wave goodbye"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Chat (aborts both ways)"
      RobotIsChatting "Another robot close by?"
      RobotChat "Wave or talk, then answer"
    Sequence AbortType=LowerPriority "Welcome home (aborts lower)"
      RobotIsWelcoming "Player back?"
      RobotWelcome "Look round, spot, come over"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Standby (aborts both ways)"
      RobotIsNight "Night?"
      RobotGoToSpot "Yawn, walk slowly to its spot"
      RobotStandby "Stand by"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Work (aborts both ways)"
      RobotHasWork "Job wants work?"
      RobotDoWork "Work"
    Sequence "Home life"
      RobotIdle "Idle, look around"
      RobotStroll "Stroll, or back to its spot"
