# The farm animals' tree (cow, sheep, chicken, pig): come home when the bell rang or the player sent it, move away from
# a scare, sleep at night where they stand, otherwise graze (in the pen, or on the pasture once the pen gate released them).
# Job: no fight. They earn Tokens, more out on the pasture, and make the player come home in time to ring the bell.
# Aware of: the player close by (a grazing animal looks up and turns to them; walked into, it steps aside) and gunshots
# (ShotFired: it runs from them, even out of its sleep). Never of enemies.
# Told by the herd (AnimalHerd.Orders, no perception): the pen gate opened = released, the bell = called home; by the
# player (Animal.SentHome, E on it): into the pen and stay there until let out; the world clock's waking hours
# (AnimalSettings.Awake) decide the night.
# Then: released, each picks a grazing spot on the pasture (a flock follower near where its leader is heading), walks
# there, eats a while (a follower moves on once its leader is gone); called or sent, it walks into the pen at its walking
# pace, trotting once the player hurries it (E after the bell, one animal at a time).
# Warns: its call now and then; every animal answers the bell; a louder one when hurried; a scream when shot at. A coin
# over it means it holds Tokens to collect (C).
# Gives up: a called animal that can't get in (the pen gate or the home gate is shut) waits by the pen gate and looks
# again every few seconds; at night it sleeps where it is unless the bell rang or something scared it.
# The three aborts (Both) re-check their conditional every think: going home cuts into everything below it, a scare into
# sleeping and grazing, the night into grazing; each branch ends as soon as its conditional turns false.
# Build: unity command script run --file .agents/skills/aipocalypse-enemy-ai/scripts/TreeKit.cs --entry TreeKit.Build
#        --args '["AgentScripts/Animals/Animal.tree.txt"]'
# Rebuilding keeps the asset's GUID; edits made in the Behavior Designer window are overwritten, so make them here.
tree Assets/_Game/Content/Animals/_Shared/Animal.asset
Repeater "Repeat forever"
  Selector "Come home, else move away, else sleep, else graze"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Come home (aborts both ways)"
      CalledHome "Bell rang or sent home, and not home?"
      WalkHome "Walk into the pen"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Move away (aborts both ways)"
      IsStartled "Scared or walked into?"
      MoveAway "Run or step away"
    Sequence AbortType=Both "Sleep (aborts both ways)"
      IsNight "Night?"
      SleepHere "Sleep where it stands"
    Sequence "Graze"
      ChooseGrazeSpot "Choose a spot"
      WalkToGrazeSpot "Walk to it"
      GrazeHere "Eat"
