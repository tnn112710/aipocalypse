# Text Studio samples

Start with `00_Entry`, then choose a focused lesson. Scenes contain persisted examples that can be inspected before entering Play Mode.

| Folder | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `00_Entry` | An immediate overview of the main capabilities. |
| `01_Appearance` | Scenes that explore lettering, materials and visual design. |
| `02_Effects` | Scenes that explore reveal, motion and interaction. |
| `03_ProductionExamples` | Distinct application examples and reusable production configurations. |
| `04_CompleteDemo` | A complete playable example. |
| `Shared` | Assets used by multiple samples. |

The distinct production scene is Text Magnifier. Combat Feedback, Responsive HUD, Accessible Captions and Karaoke Styles are in the Effects track; Production Examples retains their reusable assets. Whisperwood is the complete gameplay demo.

Reusable configurations are kept in named `Recipes` folders or production prefabs. Scripts support native playback and genuine interaction; builders belong to Text Studio Debug. `SampleCoverage.md` describes the current lessons and `MigrationLedger.md` maps retired demonstrations to their retained destinations.

Spatial Fields shows four native behaviors together. Its [recipe groups](02_Effects/Recipes/Spatial%20Fields/README.md) retain every field shape, coordinate space and blend without a settings menu; drag the target or use its keyboard navigation.

Placement and Deformation keeps native route and reward examples visible together. Its [path and deformation recipes](02_Effects/Recipes/Placement%20and%20Deformation/README.md) compare both orientations and source lengths without menu controls.

Composed Effects presents native curve and playback comparisons together. Its [recipe groups](02_Effects/Recipes/Composed%20Effects/README.md) retain all one hundred compositions and six reveal orders; the curve display observes native playback.

Expressive Motion shows reactions and tagged, whole-line and unaffected text together. Its [recipe groups](02_Effects/Recipes/Expressive%20Motion/README.md) retain ten effects, three strengths and each original area/scope setting with native playback.

Reveal Timing compares letters, words and lines beside the traveler illustration. Its [recipe groups](02_Effects/Recipes/Reveal%20Timing/README.md) retain all timing models, directions and final pauses, plus instant words, speed and disappearance. A small observer reports native events without owning configuration.

[Renderer Comparison](02_Effects/Renderers/README.md) shows two Canvas backends and three UI Toolkit source routes. Effects are authored in the scene and UXML; live input, data connections and responsive layout work through native bindings.
