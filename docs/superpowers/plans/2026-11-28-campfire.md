# Campfire Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** the yard campfire becomes a tended fire (wood → fuel hours → light and sanity safety), on a darker night, with a GA VFX flame, flicker, crackle, a fuel bar and saves.

**Architecture:** a new scene system `_Core/CampfireSystem/` (installer, settings asset, plain-C# fuel rules, the `Campfire` and `WoodPile` components, a ticker on `WorldClock` hours, a save section, console commands) plus a wood item (`MaterialDefinition`, new `ItemCategory.Material`), a HUD fuel bar, and look / grade changes baked into COZY. Content is built by `AgentScripts/Campfire/CampfireSetup.cs`.

**Tech Stack:** Unity 6000.6.11f1, URP 17.6 Forward+, VContainer, MessagePipe, BroAudio via `AudioService`, VFX Graph 17.6, A* 5.4 (`NavmeshCut`), NUnit EditMode.

**Spec:** [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-28-campfire-design.md](../specs/2026-11-28-campfire-design.md)

## Global Constraints

- Numbers (user): a log = **2 h** of fuel, max fuel **8 h** (4 logs); lit area **~20 m**; sanity-safe radius **30 m**; below **25 %** fuel the flame, lit area and safe radius shrink gradually to 0; rain burns × **1.25 / 1.5 / 2** (light / medium / heavy); new game: pit **full and unlit**; yard pile **8** logs, refilled to 8 each morning (Dawn); wood **1 kg**; night ambient × **0.3**, moon × **0.5**, −0.5 EV (Night, Midnight), half of that at Blue hour; fire never goes out at dawn by itself.
- Wood is never held: E at the pit adds one log from the inventory; wood thrown / dropped into the pit burns. Tap E = add a log (carrying wood and room for a whole log) else light (unlit, fuel > 0); hold E = put out (lit; the fuel stays).
- AGENTS.md: tests first; `AudioService` only; `InputService` only; built-in `Dictionary` serialization, no Odin serialization; save only what you wrote (`SaveAssetIfDirty`); new `SoundID`s approved by the user for the fire; docs in the same change; no commit.
- A strict `GameLifetimeScope` errors for any Core `SceneInstaller` it lacks: add `CampfireInstaller` to WorldMap in the same task that creates it.

## Review Focus

1. Sleep skip through a lit fire: fuel burns at the skip rate and the fire goes out mid-skip; the `SafeLight` turns off with it (covered in Task 3 tests: `Advance` across 0).
2. A thrown stack of wood bigger than the room left: only whole logs that fit burn, the rest stays a pickup (Task 4 test on `Campfire.Feed`).
3. Loading a save where the fire was lit: restored silently (no ignite sound), visuals and `SafeLight` match (Task 6 round-trip + `Campfire.RestoreSaved` test).
4. Weather absent (prototype scenes) or a non-rain sky: burn × 1 (Task 3 test with no entry).
5. Strict scope and scenes without the installer: the fire is a prop, nothing throws (Task 4: `CampfireInstaller` on WorldMap; `CampfireHud` hides with no registry).
