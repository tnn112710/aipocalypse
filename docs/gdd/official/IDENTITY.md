# Identity

Finalized: 2026-11-21 · Draft and open details: [ONE_PAGE.md](../ONE_PAGE.md)

What the game is called, what kind of game it is, who it is for and how it is sold.

## Title

- Release title: **Hasta la Vista, M.A.Y.B.E** (Model for Analyzing Your Behavior and Experience). Chosen 2026-11-08.
- **aipocalypse** is the development codename and namespace: repo, folders, C# namespaces, Unity product name.
- Logo: "MAYBE" is the biggest word, so the short form doesn't read as the crowded "Hasta la Vista".

## Genre

- A first-person shooter with farming, exploration and home defense.
- A midcore survival shooter with a home to tend: short, tense fights between calm days.

## Audience

- PC players who like midcore survival shooters with a home to tend.

## Platform and business

- PC, sold on Steam.
- Price: $8–12.
- Release model: Early Access first, after 6–9 months of solo development. Regions, bosses and endings grow through Early Access updates shaped by player feedback.
