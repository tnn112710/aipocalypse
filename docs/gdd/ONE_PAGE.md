# One-Page Design — aipocalypse

The whole game on one page, after Unity's [one-page game design document](https://unity.com/blog/one-page-game-design-document). Detail lives in [CORE.md](CORE.md) and [FARM_AND_ECONOMY.md](FARM_AND_ECONOMY.md); if they disagree, they win and this page gets fixed. The scope, "done" and milestone sections are **proposals to confirm**, not decisions yet.

**Title:** aipocalypse (working title)

**Concept:** A burned-out game dev wakes up inside a broken AI-made sandbox, builds a small farm in a safe meadow, explores across the river for loot, and defends home with limited ammo on raid nights.

**Core loop:** farm → explore → prepare → defend → sleep.

**Goal, win, lose**
- Goal: grow the farm and home, survive the raids, beat the Blood Moon minibosses (every 10 days) and the eclipse boss (every 30th day).
- Win: endless for now; each won major event is the milestone. The grand-trial ending is on hold.
- Lose: never final. A knockout or death costs carried loot, coins and maybe animals; deposits are safe. A lost raid leaves broken fences and stolen animals (3 days to get them back).

**Controls (PC):** WASD move · mouse look · LMB fire / swing · RMB aim · R reload · E interact (hold: repair) · Space jump · Shift sprint · C crouch · 1–9 / wheel switch item · G drop.

**Pillars:** build something worth protecting · choose how to spend the day · prepare before dark · every bullet counts · from office worker to survivor.

**Can't ship without** *(proposal)*
- First-person shooting with limited ammo (a few guns) and melee as fallback.
- Day/night clock; raid and quiet nights known each morning; Blood Moon and eclipse.
- Goblin raids that both steal and attack; breakable fences and gate with repair.
- Animals that roam, drop produce, come home at the bell and can be stolen; crops; fishing.
- Trading robot with daily stock, safe deposits, the daily box, upgrades.
- Bridge repair and an outer zone with loot and wandering threats.
- HP, hunger, stamina, sanity; sleep restores sanity.
- Save/load, knockout and death losses, a wake-up intro and first-raid tutorial.

**If time allows** *(proposal)*: the dog, collector robot, crafting, cooking beyond basics, weather, gun fishing, storage station, river-swim upgrades, more enemy kinds (robots, zombies), outer zone after dark (the Dark, mystical entities).

**Not this version** *(proposal)*: XP skills, perk cards, Luck, the cat, the grand trial and lottery endings, a cozy mode without fights, player biome portals, multiplayer, languages other than English.

**Look and sound:** stylized nature (Raygeas main look) — warm, cozy meadow by day, frightening moonlit nights where enemies stay readable. Cute service robots, a comic guide drone. Guns get the most audio work (punchy, polished); ambience follows the clock (dawn chorus, wind, owls); music TBD.

**Inspiration**
- Escape from Duckov — prepare at home, risk what you carry, home facilities.
- Thronefall / Kingdom Two Crowns — invest by day, defend a readable night.
- 7 Days to Die / Terraria — Blood Moons and eclipses players prepare for.
- Slime Rancher — physical produce and home trading.

**Who it's for:** PC players who like midcore survival shooters with a home to tend, and want short, tense fights between calm days. Steam, $8–12.

**Done means** *(proposal)*
- A new player goes from the wake-up intro through the first eclipse (day 30) without help or blocking bugs.
- Every "can't ship without" item works at its polish level ([ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md#polish-levels)).
- 60 fps at 1080p on an RTX 2060; saves are never lost.
- Five outside players finish a 30-day run and we act on their feedback.

**Milestones** *(proposal, dates to set; target 6–9 months solo)*
1. **Core feel** — movement, shooting, enemies, one raid night in WorldMap (current).
2. **One full day** — farm, animals, trading, daily box, sleep, raid and quiet nights; a day can be played start to finish.
3. **Ten days** — bridge, outer zone, upgrades, losses, save/load, the first Blood Moon.
4. **Thirty days** — eclipse boss, tutorial, balance, menus; playtest, then the Steam build.
