# Meadow Home environment

Scene: `Assets/_Game/Scenes/Prototype/Prototype_Environment.unity`. Layout: concept v13 (`docs/concepts/meadow-home-top-down-v13.png`, notes in `meadow-home-map.md`). Art target: the Raygeas packs (Komorebi Nature, HIGHLANDS, Grasslands, Lakefell Village) with BK Pure Nature as support; reference screenshots in `docs/concepts/refs/`.

Everything is generated by editor scripts in `AgentScripts/MeadowHome/` (outside `Assets/`, run with `unity command run_script`), so the layout can be changed and rebuilt. Data and first-party asset copies live in `Assets/_Game/Data/Environment/MeadowHome/`.

## World layout

One terrain, 1000 × 300 × 1000 m at the origin, heightmap 2049 (about 0.49 m), alphamap 2048, detail resolution 1024. North is +Z. The playable area is the `Playable` polygon in the generator (about X/Z 160–855); outside it is backdrop.

| Area | Where (X, Z) | Heights (world Y) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home base | fenced 30 × 30 m at (326, 274) | flat pad 28.5 m |
| Meadow | south strip, x 236–545 | 23–30 m rolling |
| River | waterfall pond (219, 428) → lake (535, 292) | surface 23.0 → 19.5 m, 2.8 m deep, shelved banks |
| Waterfall | sheet pivot (206, 433), heading 100° | lip 41.6 m → pond 23.0 m |
| Bridge | (312, 362), 89 m from the base | Grasslands `Bridge` ×1.25, square to the flow |
| Great Lake | south-east, x 506–843 | surface 19.5 m, up to 9.7 m deep, four islands, sand coves |
| Pine forest | centre and east | 24–48 m, knolls and stands with clearings |
| Highlands | north-west | 40–83 m, rock ledges, ponds at 50 m (192, 640) and 44 m (386, 806) |
| Swamp | north-east, x 527–790, z 612–803 | water 24.5 m, pools up to 3.6 m, grassy hummocks |
| Streams | West, Forest, North, Margin, Swamp outlet | 0.7–1.0 m deep; cascades down from the highlands |
| Lakeside viewpoint | (546, 246) | rock perch on the lake's south-west shore |

Player spawn: inside the yard by the campfire (333.5, 281.5), facing the gate and the bridge.

## How it is built (`MeadowHomeLayout.cs` + `Atmosphere.cs`)

Order of a full rebuild (entry points of `MeadowHomeLayout.cs` unless noted):

1. `Generate(2048, 1024, preview)`: analytic height field → `Heightmaps/MH_Base.png` (R16) plus one mask per Rowlan detail stamp. Polygons and water lines at the top of the file are the layout; edit them there.
   - Water is carved into the field: basins (lake, ponds) and lines (river, streams) with a shelved channel profile; banks never dip below the water surface.
   - Rowlan detail stamps (`Details` table: Highlands 09/07, Meadows 05/16/26, Swamp 12) are `Add` stamps at Y = 0. The shader clamps stamp heights at 0, so the base map is lowered by `median × amplitude × mask` to make them zero-mean.
2. `BuildHeights()`: the MicroVerse `Height` group: macro base (Override) → region detail → home pad.
3. Kit `MicroVerseKit.Rebuild(false)` to sync heights before anything samples the terrain.
4. `GenerateTextureMasks(1024)`, `BuildTerrainLayers()`, `BuildTextures()`: 8 first-party layer copies (Grasslands grass/blue/yellow/mud/mudstones/sand, wet mud, a Komorebi rock-detail layer) and 16 texture stamps: base grass, cool patches, forest dirt, warm meadow, highland scree, swamp grass/mud, water beds, shore pebbles, beach coves, channel beds and banks, steep rock, highland shelves, trails. Bed/shore follow the real waterline, so islands stay grassy.
5. `Vegetation.GenerateMasks(1024)`, `Vegetation.MakeVariants()`, `Vegetation.Build()`: the MicroVerse `Vegetation` group (details below).
6. `BuildBoundary(1337)`: per-side foot rocks, ridges, peaks, haze and a backdrop ground ring (models, no terrain raise) plus invisible walls. `BoundaryAngles(3)` reports, per viewpoint and 10° bearing, whether the peaks rise above the layers in front.
7. `BuildWaterMaterials()`, `BuildFlatWater()`, `BuildRivers()`: Stylized Water 3 (below).
8. `Landmarks.Build(7)`, `Landmarks.BuildWaterfall(206, 433, 100, 41.6, 23)`, `Outcrops.Build(5)`, `Dressing.BuildWaterEdges(21)`, `Dressing.BuildBanks(23)`, `Finishing.Islands(9)`.
9. `Atmosphere.cs`: `ClonePostProcessing()`, `Apply()`, `Particles()`, `SharedWind()`; then `Vegetation.Wind()` (also adds `BK Foliage Globals`), `Finishing.FillLight(0.45)`, `Finishing.PlayerSpawn()`.
10. Kit `Rebuild(true)` (terrain trees exist from here), then `Dressing.BuildMeadow(31)` (keeps its stones and hero trees off terrain trees), `Vegetation.GenerateClearMasks(1024)` (after every rock or dressing pass), kit `Rebuild(true)` + `SaveScene("")`.
11. `OpenScene()` reloads the saved scene. Do this before screenshots: the GPU Resident Drawer keeps drawing objects that scripts deleted or disabled until the scene reloads.

The scripted object groups (rocks, dressing, landmarks) sample terrain heights when placed: rebuild them after reshaping the terrain. MicroVerse stamps regenerate by themselves. `Verify(outDir)` checks basin depths, shore rims, channel depths, region heights and slopes, and the home pad. `Delta()` shows how much the detail stamps move each region.

## Boundary

No terrain is raised for mountains: mountain meshes stand in for raised far ground. Each side uses one asset family, in layers that each rise above the one in front. (The earlier Komorebi rock rows stood 30–120 m tall and hid the peaks.)

| Layer | Distance beyond the playable edge | Height above local ground |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foot rocks (clusters of 2–6, about 35% of each side bare) | 0–30 m (west terraces to 130 m) | 2–8 m (at most 3 m near the home and viewpoint); west 6–30 m |
| Tree belt (MicroVerse `Rim` masks) | 4–175 m | trees 9–27 m; short trees behind the home |
| Ridges | 250–400 m | 60–130 m |
| Peaks | 550–800 m (east far range about 1.1 km) | 260–450 m |

| Side | Foot rocks | Ridges | Peaks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| South (behind the home) | HIGHLANDS `Forest/Rock_1/2` (+Grass) | HIGHLANDS `Mountain_4` (green; XZ 0.4, Y 0.6) | HIGHLANDS `Mountain_1/2/3` |
| West (waterfall side) | Komorebi `Rocks Grass/Rock_1-3` and NoGrass terraces | Komorebi `Rocks Snow` plateaus (scale 4–7, tilted, sunk) | HIGHLANDS `Mountain_1/2` |
| North | Grasslands `Clif_1-3` slabs | BK Meadows `Mountain1/2`, flattened (XZ 22,000, Y about 10,000) | Grasslands `Mountain_1/2` cones |
| East (beyond the lake) | Lakefell `Rock_1/2/3` | BK Meadows hills | HIGHLANDS `Mountain_3` focal peak; BK Mountains `Mountain1/2` far range |

- Corners mix the two neighbouring families: each cluster picks its side from the outward normal with ±25° of jitter.
- HIGHLANDS mountain tiles are whole ranges with a low side (M1 −Z, M2 +Z, M3 +X, M4 −Z). Yaw = direction to the map − low side, which turns the gentle slopes toward the player.
- Mountains sit 4 m below the lowest ground under their footprint. They are pushed outward until no visible vertex is within 40 m of the playable area. Shadows are off.
- The HIGHLANDS and Grasslands mountain shaders ignore scene fog; their own height haze (`_FogColor`) is teal. `Boundary/MH_Boundary_*.mat` are copies retinted to the sky haze; the vendor materials are untouched. Scene fog is 0.0011, so near layers don't look hazier than the peaks.
- `Haze`: Komorebi `Fog_1` banks at the feet of ridges and peaks.
- `Backdrop Ground`: a ring mesh from the terrain border out to 2.5 km, just under the border heights, so no gap between meshes shows the void.
- Invisible walls: 176 box colliders along the playable edge on `Ignore Raycast` (they stop the player, not shots). Keep-out zones keep rocks off the home base, viewpoint, waterfall lip and pond, bridge and streams.
- The player camera's far clip is 1500 m: keep peaks within about 1.4 km of the viewpoints.

## Water (Stylized Water 3)

- Flat bodies (lake, three ponds, swamp, the waterfall's source pool) are `WaterObject`s on meshes generated from the shore outlines: 2 m cells kept only where at least one corner is under water and not beside a lower stream. Transform Y is the water level. Meshes: `Water/MH_*.asset`.
- Rivers and streams are `RiverModeler` ribbons (`Environment/Water/Rivers`) on the same knots that carved the beds, 25% wider than the channel plus 0.8 m so the edges tuck under the banks. Each line ends a few metres inside the water body it joins.
- Materials: `Water/MH_Water_Still.mat` (from SW3 Smooth) and `MH_Water_River.mat` (from SplineRiver Default), tinted to Komorebi's turquoise. River mode needs vertex-colour transparency and foam on and intersection source 2.
- Calm flow: the vendor river material runs time at 16×. Ours uses `_Speed` 6, slope speed 1.5, foam speed 0.7 and ribbon foam velocity strength 0.45. The waterfall copy flows at 0.42×.
- Intersection source 2 adds the ribbon's vertex-colour edge band to the foam. In sharp mode everything above the clip turns white, which drew a continuous curb along both banks. `_IntersectionClipping` 0.6 keeps only the noise peaks. Don't set `_IntersectionLength` below 1: that zeroes the depth term.
- Gotcha: refreshing a mesh asset with `EditorUtility.CopySerialized` left the old GPU buffers on screen; `SaveMesh` refills it through the Mesh API instead.

## Waterfall

Komorebi kit on rock meshes, scaled from the Komorebi demo: `Waterfall_1` (sy = drop / 6.2, a material copy `Water/MH_Waterfall.mat` at queue 3001 so it draws after SW3 water), `Waterfall_Ripples_1`, `Waterfall_Splash_1`, `Fog_1`, a blue point light sunk in the pool floor, `Rock_1_NoGrass` lip slabs and grass-topped notch walls. Re-run `BuildWaterfall` with a new pivot/heading to move it.

## Vegetation (MicroVerse stamps)

Masks are terrain-space R16 maps on whole-terrain stamps (stamp UV = terrain UV): `MH_Veg_Forest/Meadow/MeadowFlowers/Hedge/Highlands/Swamp/Foothills/Wild/Reeds/WaterEdge/Rim/RimBroadleaf/RimShort/RimBushes`. Grass and flowers also use a texture filter (grass layers only), so they stay off dirt, sand and rock. Water distances for masks use the carve's noisy channel width and shoreline (`WaterEdgeDistancePublic`, `TrueLineEdge`), so the bands follow the real waterline.

- **Trees** (TreeStamps):
  - Forest and foothills: Komorebi pines with Grasslands broadleaf and birch groves.
  - Highlands only: HIGHLANDS firs (they read cyan next to the Komorebi pines). The tall firs use `Vegetation/Prefabs/Fir_Tall_*_Capsule.prefab`, variants with a CapsuleCollider, because terrain trees ignore MeshColliders.
  - Meadow: Grasslands broadleafs and birches as singles and small groups.
  - Rim belt outside the playable edge: Komorebi pines, Grasslands large broadleafs (north-east, east, some south), and short trees and bushes behind the home.
  - About 3,800 instances.
- **Bushes** (TreeStamps): Komorebi flowering bushes in clumps on the meadow; hedgerows (Grasslands `Bush_Large/Medium`, Komorebi bushes) on the meadow rim and the viewpoint path; forest understory, foothill, highland, swamp and rim bushes.
- **Meadow**, after the BK Pure Nature Meadows demo:
  - A short BK lawn (`Plants/Grass1`, `Grass2`, aligned to the ground).
  - Flower heads speckled in loose patches (`Daisy`, `CommonBlue`, `CommonPink`), large lavender drifts (`Lavender`), orange and blue fields (`FlowerMeadow1/3`), gorse accents (`Gorse1`), pale barley tufts (`GrassMeadows3`).
  - Komorebi tall grass only at tree feet.
  - Hard-edged drifts come from a high noise amplitude (8–12) with a per-stamp `offset`. `MeadowFlowers` keeps flowers off the yard (a ragged round fade, 22–38 m) and the trails.
  - The meadow terrain layer is tinted to the BK lawn green.
- **Other ground cover** (DetailStamps, coverage mode): wild grass, tufts, flowers and clovers outside the meadow; ferns and needle litter near trees; reeds in clumps at the waterline of lakes, ponds, the swamp, the river (not by the bridge) and the slow margin stream; three Komorebi ferns and farfugium along the water; highland scree; swamp marsh grass.
- Unity's detail system rejects prefabs with a LODGroup on the mesh root. `Vegetation/Details/*_Detail.prefab` are plain mesh copies for those (Komorebi `Grass_1_Large`, HIGHLANDS grass, flowers and clovers). BK plants have no LODGroup and are used directly.
- BK plants need `BK Foliage Globals` (`_Core/EnvironmentSystem/BKFoliageGlobals`, on `Environment/Wind`). Without it they fade out beyond about 50 m and have no micro wind.
- **Clearing**: two ClearStamps last in the group, masked by `MH_Veg_ClearTrees` / `MH_Veg_ClearDetails`. `GenerateClearMasks` builds them from the scene: footprints of outcrops, waterfall rocks, boundary foot rocks, meadow stones, bank and shore rocks, the yard, the house and props, the bridge, and trail cores. Re-run it after moving rocks or landmarks.
- Noise (MicroVerse FBM): n spans about ±0.47 (p10/p90 ±0.14); weight = saturate(1 + (n − balance) × amplitude). Tree stamps keep each candidate with probability = weight.
- Rendering: Unity's terrain renderer (tree distance 5000, detail distance 80). GPU Instancer Pro is not set up yet; see "Open decisions".

## Rocks and dressing (scripted GameObjects)

- `Rock Outcrops`: highland ledges (rows of 2–4 grass-topped cliff rocks, tops flush with the uphill ground), the lakeside viewpoint perch, about 95 boulder clusters.
- `Dressing/Water Edges` (lake and pond shores only): stone clusters (Komorebi SmallStone 1–4, 35% of the shoreline left bare), pebbles, and lily groups in 0.9–2.2 m of calm water so the stems end in the bed, including the river mouth.
- `Dressing/Bank Rocks` (`BuildBanks`) walks every river and stream line on both banks, about 1,550 objects:
  - Each sample gets a bank character from the bend (outer bank = cut) plus alternating bars. Cut banks are rock-lined; bars have few stones.
  - Lining stones come in runs of 4–14 m with gaps: Komorebi SmallStones on the river and forest streams, HIGHLANDS stones in the highlands.
  - Set-back boulders, stones in the channel and driftwood.
  - A grass-topped rock wall on the cut bank by the bridge.
  - Fallen-log footbridges (Komorebi `Log_2`) where the forest→swamp and highland-spur trails cross streams.
- `Dressing/Meadow Stones` and `Meadow Trees` (`BuildMeadow`): BK `Rocks/Stone1-4` in 18 low clusters half-sunk in the lawn, three grass-topped Komorebi stones, and lone Grasslands `Broadleaf_Large` hero trees away from the fence, trails and terrain trees.
- `Dressing/Islands`: pines, bushes and stone rings on the four lake islands.
- `Landmarks`: Lakefell `House_1` (door faces the gate), 30 × 30 m Lakefell fence with a gate, campfire (HIGHLANDS Bonfire + Fire FX with audio off and shadows off) with benches and a stone ring, yard props, bridge, trail sign.
- Terrain texture along water: channel beds and banks are wet mud with grey pebble patches (`MH_Pebbles` uses Komorebi's `Stones_1` textures). Lake and pond shore pebbles stop 0.15–0.6 m above the water, so grass meets the shore.

## Atmosphere

- Post-processing: global Volume `Post-Processing (Komorebi grade)` → `MH_PostProcessing.asset` (copy of Komorebi's demo grade, toned down: contrast 20, saturation +2, white balance +10, bloom 0.3, vignette 0.25; lens flare and motion blur off).
- The player prefab renders no post-processing. This scene overrides `WeaponCamera` (the last camera in the stack) to `renderPostProcessing = true`, so world and weapon get one grade. The Player prefab asset is unchanged.
- Sun 48° / 300°, 5400 K, 2.5, soft shadows, Komorebi cloud-shadow cookie (500 m) drifting with `Raygeas.CloudMover`. A shadowless sky-bounce fill light from the north lifts the north-facing cliffs.
- Sky `MH_Skybox.mat` (Komorebi painted sky, rotation 17 so its sun glow sits behind the light). Fog ExponentialSquared 0.0011, (0.58, 0.76, 0.94). Trilight ambient.
- `Environment/Atmosphere`: 24 god rays aligned to the sun, mist over clearings, the lake, river and stream mouths, swamp fog, 10 cloud particles around the rim, high and local bird flocks, butterflies over the meadow, leaf fall under the broadleafs.
- `Environment/Wind`: the three Raygeas wind drivers at yaw 30 (sun yaw + 90), and `BK Foliage Globals` for the BK plants.

## Open decisions and known issues

- **GPUI.** Project rules say GPU Instancer Pro draws foliage. Doing that needs `_Game` copies of the Raygeas Shader Graphs with GPUI's setup node (otherwise magenta), and a decision on GPUI's generated `*_GPUIPro.shader` files next to vendor shaders. Until then the spawn view in play mode costs about 2.5k draw calls, 2.4M triangles, 15 ms CPU and 7 ms GPU (Unity terrain trees).
- **Editor: GPU Resident Drawer.** Objects that scripts delete or disable keep being drawn until the scene reloads (`OpenScene()`). Don't force a domain reload (`EditorUtility.RequestScriptReload`) to refresh it. Right after MicroVerse work, a forced reload left the TerrainData's managed wrapper pointing at another object, and the next scene save crashed the editor.
- After the scene opens, GRD logs "BatchDrawCommand ... invalid Mesh" errors for `MH_Water_River` until RiverModeler regenerates the ribbon meshes. They stop after a few seconds.
- **Player builds.** Raygeas `Shared Assets/Scripts/WindController.cs` and `WindSettings.cs` have an unguarded `using UnityEditor;`.
- **Vendor material churn.** Unity re-validates some vendor materials when their prefabs are instanced (`_FADING_ON` dropped from HIGHLANDS `Fire.mat` and Komorebi `GodRays.mat`; COZY editor assets touched). Revert those diffs; don't commit them.
- Far vistas: trees drop at their LOD cull distance, so the forest thins when viewed from over ~350 m (GPUI billboards would fix it).
- Not built yet: chimney smoke (Lakefell's smoke uses a built-in shader), fish (Komorebi's are koi), A* rescan for this terrain, baked lighting/reflection probes.
