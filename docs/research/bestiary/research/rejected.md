# Rejected and near-miss packs

Considered during the sweep and left out of `store_packs.json`. Prices are current USD on 2026-11-08. "Not animatable" = static mesh, or a Generic rig without its own idle/move/attack/hit/death clips. Excluded publishers (Synty, Magic Pig / Infinity PBR, Polysplit, Dungeon Mason, Agor2012, Meshtint, GrigoriyArx) were skipped and are not listed.

## Gaps found

- No stylized dilophosaurus-style dino spitter passes the rules. The only dilophosaurs (Vipera, Muelsa) are realistic. Use a raptor body plus a spit VFX, or the listed Downrain VenomBlossom / Carnivorous Plant as the spitter.
- No affordable stylized shark or piranha enemy with combat clips beyond what is owned (polyperfect shark).
- No turret creature with hit/death clips in a Synty-like look. Mech Legion's Gunner/Salvo and the RadialMoon walkers are the closest.
